Support

Help for Scrap King on iPhone and iPad · Email austin@carlinkhq.com and we answer within two business days

Fastest way to get help Email austin@carlinkhq.com with your device model, iOS version, the Game version (shown at the bottom of the Game's Settings sheet) and what happened. Screenshots help a lot.

Purchases

I bought something and did not get it

Open the Shop or Settings inside the Game and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. Non-consumable items (No Ads, Foreman bundle, Auto-crane) restore this way. If Yard Cash did not arrive, force-quit the Game and reopen it; the transaction finishes on the next launch. Still missing? Email us with the receipt from Apple and we will sort it out.

I want a refund

App Store purchases are refunded by Apple, not by us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Does No Ads remove every ad?

No Ads removes the interstitial ads that play between truck loads. The optional rewarded videos (2× cash, finish an upgrade, double a bonus) stay available because they only play when you tap to watch one.

Progress

Does my yard sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes, if iCloud Drive is on for your Apple Account on both devices. Progress is mirrored through iCloud; it can take a minute after you switch devices. There are no accounts or passwords.

I lost my progress

Progress lives on the device and in iCloud. If you deleted the app and reinstalled it, open the Game once with iCloud Drive turned on and it will pull the last saved yard. If you play on a device that is signed out of iCloud, the yard is local to that device only.

How do offline earnings work?

When you close the Game, the crew keeps the active yard ticking over at a reduced rate for up to 20 minutes, and every extra lot you own keeps earning at its full foreman rate. Auto-crane extends the window to 2 hours. You collect it on the "While you were out" screen the next time you open the Game.

Can I reset my yard?

Delete the Game from your device and, if you use iCloud, remove its data under Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage before reinstalling. There is no in-game reset because purchases and progress are stored together.

Ads

The 2× button or "Watch an ad" option is missing

Rewarded ads only appear once an ad is loaded and ready. That depends on your connection and on ad availability in your region; try again in a minute. If you declined the ad consent form in the EU or UK, no ads are requested at all; you can change that under Ad privacy choices in the Game's Settings.

How do I stop personalized ads?

iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, turn off Scrap King. Ads keep working, they are just not personalized with your advertising identifier. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Gameplay

Why won't the magnet lift something?

Each magnet tier lifts a set of scrap. A piece that shivers when the magnet is over it needs the next Magnet power upgrade. Very large scrap also needs floor space: the yard level shown in Upgrades tells you when the trucks will start bringing it.

What are gold pieces?

Every so often a gold-plated piece shows up in a load. It is worth ten times the normal value and only the magnet can pick it up; forklifts and the sorter belt leave it for you.

The Game runs slowly or gets warm

Scrap King renders a 3D yard at 60 frames per second. Closing other apps, turning off Low Power Mode, and keeping iOS up to date all help. Supported devices run iOS 17 or later.
