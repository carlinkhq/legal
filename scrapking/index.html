Scrap King

Scrapyard crane game for iPhone and iPad, published by CarLink HQ LLC

Run a scrapyard from a tower crane: swing the magnet over the pile, drop the load in the crusher, cash the cubes and grow the yard from cans and bike frames to buses, airliners and yachts. Five sites, a goal ladder, daily streaks, gold pieces and a crew that keeps working while you are away.
