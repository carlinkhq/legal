Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026 · Version 1.0 · Applies to the Scrap King app for iPhone and iPad

The short version Scrap King has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email, or contact details. Your game progress is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. Purchases go through Apple. The game shows ads served by Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads; you can limit that on your device at any time. That is the whole picture, and the rest of this page spells it out.

1. Who we are

Scrap King is published by CarLink HQ LLC, a Georgia limited liability company based in Woodstock, Georgia, USA ("CarLink HQ", "we", "us"). For anything in this policy, email austin@carlinkhq.com. We are the controller for any personal data processed by the app itself; Google LLC is an independent controller for the advertising data described in Section 3.

2. What the app stores on your device

Scrap King keeps your progress (cash, upgrades, goals, streaks, settings and purchase flags) in the app's private storage on your device. If iCloud Drive is turned on for your Apple Account, a copy is mirrored through Apple's iCloud key-value storage so the same yard shows up on your other devices. We never receive this data: it lives with you and with Apple under your Apple Account. Deleting the app deletes the local copy; the iCloud copy can be removed in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Scrap King is free to download and shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob: short interstitial ads between truck loads, and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game bonus. To serve and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and app-scoped identifiers otherwise.
  • Approximate location inferred from your IP address (country and region level; the app never asks for or uses GPS location).
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped, and rewarded-video completions.
  • Device and diagnostic data: device model, OS version, language, app version, crash and performance information about the ad SDK.

Google uses this data to select and measure ads, to prevent fraud, and, where you have allowed it, to personalize ads across apps. How Google handles it is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We do not sell this data ourselves and we do not receive it in a form that identifies you; we see aggregate ad revenue reports.

Tracking permission. On first use the app asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether Google may use your device's advertising identifier. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still appear but are not personalized using that identifier. You can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Consent choices. Where the law requires it (for example the EU, UK and Switzerland, and certain US states), the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested and offers an "Ad privacy choices" option in the game's Settings so you can revisit your choice. Where the form is not shown, the tracking permission above and the iOS advertising controls are the way to limit personalization.

No Ads purchase. Buying "No Ads" removes the interstitial ads between loads. Rewarded videos remain available only if you tap to watch one. The ad SDK stays in the app so those optional videos can work.

4. Purchases

All purchases are made through Apple's App Store and are governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy. We never see your payment card, billing address or Apple Account details. The app receives a signed transaction from Apple that names the product bought and a transaction identifier, which it uses to grant the item and to restore non-consumable purchases on your other devices. That record stays on the device and with Apple; we do not run a purchase server.

5. Diagnostics

If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us aggregated, de-identified crash and usage reports. We use them only to fix bugs. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

7. Who receives data

Apple Inc. (App Store, iCloud, analytics) and Google LLC (AdMob) are the only third parties that receive data through Scrap King, each under its own privacy policy. We do not sell personal information, and we have no advertising partners other than Google. If we ever add a service that changes this, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Under some US state privacy laws, allowing Google to use your advertising identifier for personalized ads can count as "sharing" or a "sale" of personal information. You can opt out with the tracking permission and the ad privacy choices described in Section 3; we honor those choices as your opt-out.

8. International transfers

We are based in the United States and Google operates worldwide, so advertising data may be processed in the United States and other countries. Google relies on standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for transfers of European data; details are in Google's privacy documentation linked above.

9. How long data is kept

Your save data stays on your device and in your iCloud account until you delete the app or the iCloud data. Apple keeps purchase records under its own retention rules. Google's retention periods for advertising data are described in its privacy policy; Google states that most ad-server logs are anonymized within months of collection.

10. Children

Scrap King is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the ad SDK is configured to request ads suitable for general audiences. If you are under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live), please play only with a parent's permission and ask them to choose "Ask App Not to Track". If you believe a child has provided us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

11. Your rights and choices

  • Access, correction, deletion, portability, objection and restriction (EU, UK, and similar laws): because we hold no personal data about you on our own systems, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device (delete the app, delete iCloud data) or with Google and Apple. Email us anyway; we will help you route the request and answer within 30 days.
  • California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas and other US states: you have the right to know what is collected, to delete it, to opt out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. The opt-out for targeted advertising is the tracking permission and the in-game ad privacy choices. Authorized agents may contact us by email on your behalf.
  • Ad personalization: iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising; the "Ad privacy choices" row in the game's Settings where shown.
  • Complaints: you can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. We would appreciate the chance to resolve it first.

12. Security

Save data is protected by iOS app sandboxing and, for the iCloud copy, by Apple's iCloud encryption. Purchase transactions are cryptographically signed by Apple and verified on the device. No method of storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will post the new version here with a new effective date and, where the change affects how your data is used, show a notice in the app before it takes effect. Continued use of Scrap King after that date means you accept the updated policy.
